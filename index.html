Questions

Frequently asked questions

What is generative engine optimization (GEO)? +

It's the practice of getting your business to show up when people ask AI tools like ChatGPT, Gemini, and Perplexity for a recommendation. Traditional SEO helps you rank on Google's results page. GEO helps you become the answer the AI actually gives.

How do I show up in ChatGPT or AI search results? +

AI tools pull from your Google Business Profile, online listings, reviews, and the content on your website, especially content that clearly answers real questions. When those signals are consistent and easy to read, AI is far more likely to recommend you. That's exactly what I set up.

Isn't this just search engine optimization (SEO)? +

There's overlap, and a strong local SEO foundation still matters. But AI tools choose and summarize businesses differently than Google's ranked list does. GEO focuses on the signals those tools rely on, so you're not just findable, you're the one being recommended.

I already show up on Google. Do I still need this? +

Ranking on Google and being recommended by AI aren't the same thing, and plenty of businesses that rank well are invisible in ChatGPT. As more customers start their search with AI, the gap between the two is where you quietly lose business. This closes it.

Do you work with businesses like mine? +

As of now I'm only working with Hudson County businesses. Whether you're a fitness studio, a clinic, a salon, a cafe or anything else local, then yes, I'd love to work with you! These are the businesses that can benefit the most from increased AI search visibility; they tend to get overlooked, and they gain the most when it's done right.
